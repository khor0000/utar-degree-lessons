2. Pre-empted misconceptions (looks alike, is not)

Looks-like pair or wrong beliefThe difference / correctionSlide(s)
Threat vs vulnerabilityThreat = who or what could cause harm. Vulnerability = the weakness it uses. No vulnerability, nothing to exploit.16, 21
Vulnerability vs riskA vulnerability is a weakness. Risk is how serious that weakness is for the business (severity, likelihood, impact), judged in risk assessment.21, 22, 23
Virus vs wormVirus needs a human to help it spread and infects files. Worm spreads itself across the network and does not infect other programs.42, 44
Malware vs virusMalware is the umbrella term for any harmful program. A virus is one kind of malware.41
Trojan vs virusTrojan fakes a useful function to hide its bad action (concealment). Virus copies itself into other files (propagation). They are sorted by different things.41
Rootkit vs backdoorRootkit hides its own presence by changing the OS. Backdoor is the secret entry point the attacker uses to come back.36, 41
DoS vs DDoSDoS sends a single request. DDoS sends multiple requests. Both aim to make a resource unavailable.50
Phishing vs identity theftPhishing is the trick that collects the information. Identity theft is the later misuse of someone's identity.45, 46
Script kiddie vs hackerScript kiddie is inexperienced and uses pre-written tools. Hacker is skilled and intends to break in.5, 6
White vs black hatThe difference is permission and purpose, not skill. White hat has permission and reports; black hat is illegal and destructive.7, 40
Hacktivist vs cybercriminalSame tools, different motive: hacktivist = political or ideological cause; cybercriminal = profit.9, 10
Zero-day vs unpatchedZero-day = the flaw is unknown, so no patch exists. Unpatched = the flaw is known but the fix has not been applied.21, 26, 53
Cyber espionage vs APTEspionage is the goal (secret information). APT is the style of attack (targeted, long-term, stealthy) often used to reach that goal.52, 56
Attack lifecycle stage vs attack typeThe lifecycle is the order of steps inside one attack, not a list of attack types.28
