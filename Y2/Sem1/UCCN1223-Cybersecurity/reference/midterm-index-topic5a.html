2. Pre-empted misconceptions

Looks-like pair, or wrong beliefThe difference / correctionSlide
Passive vs active attackPassive: only learns or uses information, nothing is changed, and it is hard to detect. Active: alters resources or their operation. Eavesdropping is passive; a false stream is active.12–15
Attack vs mechanism vs serviceAttack = the threat action. Mechanism = the tool that counters it (for example encipherment). Service = the protection the system gives (for example confidentiality). Do not swap them.10, 29
Confidentiality vs integrityConfidentiality stops unauthorised reading (disclosure). Integrity stops unauthorised changing or destroying. A stolen unencrypted disk is a confidentiality loss.5, 19
Confidentiality vs privacyPrivacy is individual control over what is collected and who sees information about them. The deck files it under confidentiality, but it is about people’s control.5
Authentication vs access controlAuthentication proves who you are. Access control decides what you may use. Access control needs authentication first.31, 32
Authentication vs non-repudiationAuthentication convinces the receiver. Non-repudiation gives proof the receiver can show to a third party who does not trust them.31, 35
Non-repudiation (service) vs digital signature (mechanism)Non-repudiation is the goal. The digital signature is one of the mechanisms used to achieve it.22, 35
Masquerade vs modification of messagesMasquerade: pretending to be someone else. Modification: the real sender’s message is altered, delayed or reordered. Example: John changed to Fred on a file permission.16, 17
Insider vs outsider attackInsider has legitimate access but uses it in a way not approved. Outsider is unauthorised and comes from outside the perimeter.18
Traffic analysis vs traffic paddingTraffic analysis is the passive attack (reading patterns: source, destination, length, frequency). Traffic padding is the defence that adds bits to hide those patterns.14, 24, 33
Assurance vs evaluationAssurance = confidence that measures work, built into design and implementation. Evaluation = checking a finished product against criteria, often by testing.50, 51
Security policy vs security mechanismPolicy is the set of rules (what must be protected and how). Mechanisms are the technical or procedural controls that enforce it.26, 48
