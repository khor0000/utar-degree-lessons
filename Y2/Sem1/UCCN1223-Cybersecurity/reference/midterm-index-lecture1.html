2. Pre-empted misconceptions

Looks-like pair or wrong beliefThe difference / correctionSlide no
Vulnerability vs threat vs attack vs riskVulnerability is the weak spot. Threat is the danger. Attack is the intentional act. Risk is the chance of harm plus its impact. Four different words, four different jobs.18, 19
Threat vs attackA threat can be a natural disaster, which is not intentional. An attack is always intentional.18, 19
Authentication vs authorisationAuthentication = who are you (identity check). Authorisation = what you may do, decided after authentication.20
IDS vs IPSIDS detects and reports. IPS prevents and blocks. Same family, different job.20
Firewall vs IDS / IPSFirewall controls traffic by its rules. IDS only detects. IPS actively blocks. Do not swap the job words.19, 20
Cybersecurity vs cyberspaceCyberspace is the online environment. Cybersecurity is the protection of systems, networks and data in it.5, 8, 10, 18
Compliance means only lawsThe heading covers two things: data protection laws, and industry standards (ISO/IEC 27001, NIST). Name both.14
Industrial growth vs protection of industrial systemsTwo separate headings. Growth = security enables it. Protection = ICS, SCADA and IoT must be defended from attack.14, 15
History years mixed up1820 = first recorded cybercrime. 1976 = first spam email. 1982 = first virus (EIK Cloner). Each year is a different event.3
