The interrupted keyword cipher

The last keyword variant: a repeated keyword letter becomes a * instead of being dropped, and the columns are read left to right.

Rule: * for each repeat → fill row by row → read columns left to right → that's the strip.

Critical Exam Trap • Two Reading Orders

Transposed reads its columns in alphabetical order of the keyword letters. Interrupted reads them left to right, in keyword order. Using the wrong order gives a strip that looks right but decrypts to nonsense.

Correct (earns marks)Incorrect (loses marks)
Transposed: E, I, L, R, S, WTransposed read in keyword order: W, I, R, E, L, S
Interrupted: left to right, in keyword orderInterrupted read in alphabetical order, which is the transposed rule

2. Checks

Same pattern as lesson 12c: encipher the first word, decipher the second. The answers aren't real words, so check your strip, not the meaning.

Check 1 • Interrupted keyword, RESEARCH

Encipher UTAR, then decipher RUAT, with the keyword RESEARCH.

Reveal step 1: the header

RESEARCH with repeats as *: R E S * A * C H, 8 columns.

Reveal step 2: the grid

The other 20 letters, row by row: B D F G I J K L / M N O P Q T U V / W X Y Z.

Reveal step 3: the columns and strip

Read left to right: R B M W, E D N X, S F O Y, G P Z, A I Q, J T, C K U, H L V. The two * columns give only their letters, G P Z and J T. The strip is R B M W E D N X S F O Y G P Z A I Q J T C K U H L V.

Reveal step 4: UTAR enciphered

U→C, T→T, A→R, R→Q. So UTAR → CTRQ.

Reveal step 5: RUAT deciphered

R is at position 0, so A; U at 22, so W; A at 15, so P; T at 19, so T. So RUAT → AWPT.

In interrupted keyword, what happens to a keyword letter that repeats?

Yes. The * keeps the column count at the keyword length, and the * column still gets letters.
Dropping is the transposed rule. Interrupted keeps the position with a *, so the grid is 8 columns wide for WIRELESS.
