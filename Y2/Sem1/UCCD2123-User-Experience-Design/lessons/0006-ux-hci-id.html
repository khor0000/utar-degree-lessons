UX, HCI and interaction design

UX design aims at a positive experience across the whole journey, HCI studies how people interact with technology, and interaction design designs the interactive system. They overlap, but each has its own focus.

A memory hook: UX is the goal, HCI is the study, interaction design (ID) is the design.

Explore the overlap

Tap a circle or the centre.

Each circle is one field. The centre is what all three share (Lecture 1, p15).

UX. Focus: overall satisfaction and enjoyment. Scope: the entire journey, from discovery to ongoing use. Key: usability, accessibility, desirability, effectiveness.

HCI. Focus: how people interact with computers and digital devices. Scope: designing and evaluating interfaces. Key: efficiency, effectiveness, satisfaction.

Interaction design. Focus: designing interactive systems, products and services. Scope: interfaces, interactions, behaviours. Key: usability, intuitiveness, engagement.

Shared by all three: usability and user research. Interface design is shared by HCI and ID. It is the next lesson's starting point.

T1 Q5: How are UX, HCI and Interaction Design related, and how do they differ?

reveal model answer

Q5. UX, HCI and interaction design are closely related fields that share goals and overlap in usability, user research and interface design. They differ in focus. UX design is the overarching goal of a positive experience across the entire user journey. HCI is the study of how people interact with computers and digital devices. Interaction design is the design of interactive systems, including their interfaces and behaviours.
