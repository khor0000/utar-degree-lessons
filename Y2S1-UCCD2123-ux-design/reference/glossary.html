Glossary

TermMeaningLesson
User experience (UX)The whole journey a person goes through using a product, including how their view of it changes.0001
UX design (UXD)Developing interactive systems, products and services that are usable and enjoyable.0001
ProductWorking meaning: whatever you are responsible for designing.0002
JourneyWorking meaning: everything one person goes through to reach one goal. Count one per person, per goal.0002
Interactive systemTechnology that responds to people's actions and handles content they can perceive. A phone app is one; a chair is not.0001
Human-centredDesigning around people's needs, goals and contexts.0003
Machine-centred viewSeeing people as vague and machines as precise, so people must adapt to the machine.0003
PACTPeople, Activities, Contexts, Technologies: the four elements to consider when designing interactive systems.0008
PACT analysisListing the variety of each PACT element in a domain to scope a design problem.0008
RequirementsWhat a system must do and the qualities it should have.0009
OpportunitiesNew possibilities a technology offers, which change activities.0009
Device ecologySeveral devices working together to create an environment.0005
HCIHuman-computer interaction: the study of how people interact with technology.0006
Interaction designThe design of interactive systems.0006
UsabilityThe ease with which users can effectively and efficiently interact with a system to achieve their goals.0007
EffectiveThe system lets the user achieve the goal.0007
EfficientThe goal is reached with minimal effort or time.0007
TouchpointA place where a person meets a service (an app screen, a counter, a rider at the gate).Benyon ch4
InteractionWhat happens at a touchpoint: the person acts and the system responds.0006b
Service"A sequence of interactions that constitutes a whole, more abstract achievement"; intangible.Benyon ch4
Feedback (principle)The system tells people what effect their action had.Benyon ch5 principle 7
Constraint (principle)Design that blocks wrong actions, e.g. scan a QR instead of typing 16 digits.Benyon ch5 principle 9
Recovery (principle)Letting people recover from mistakes quickly, e.g. a biometric login when the PIN is forgotten.Benyon ch5 principle 8
Trade-offGaining one quality by giving up another, e.g. speed vs security.Benyon ch2 2.6
